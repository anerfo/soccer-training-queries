# Weekly training polls.
#
# Format, one poll per line:
#   Question | First answer | Second answer | Optional third answer
#
# - Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
# - Answers are separated by "|". Keep at least two answers.
# - Polls are sent in order, then the list wraps around to the top.
# - Edit this file anytime; no daemon restart is needed.
#
# Examples:

Training this week - are you joining? | Yes | No | Maybe
Who's in for this week's session? | I'm in | Can't make it
Are you available for training? | Yes | No | Only later
Count yourself in for training? | Count me in | Not this week
Training attendance - are you coming? | Yes | No | Maybe
Will you make it to the session? | Yes | No | I'll be late
Who's showing up this week? | Me | Not me | Unsure
Any chance you'll join training? | Definitely | Probably | No
Weekly training check-in: are you in? | Yes | No | Maybe
Ready for training? | Yes | No | Need a reminder
